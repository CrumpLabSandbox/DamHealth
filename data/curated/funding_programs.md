## Programs seen but not fully characterized

- **Utah Division of Water Resources dam safety funding** – ~$3.8M/yr state appropriation plus $7.2M FEMA HHPD (FY2024); 107 of 227 high-hazard dams need rehab. Specific award list not found.
- **Oklahoma Water Resources Board dam grants** – $1M grant to OKC Water Utilities Trust for Lake Overholser design (2024); 2025 Comprehensive Water Plan recommends one-time $15M for watershed dam rehab (not appropriated).
- **Pennsylvania (DCNR state parks dams / H2O PA / Growing Greener)** – DCNR has 139 dams (46 high-hazard needing work) using ARPA and capital funds; H2O PA "High Hazard Unsafe Dams" grants (2019 list). No 2024–2026 program-level figures found.
- **Ohio ODNR dam rehabilitation (capital budget)** – $50M requested for FY2027–2032 incl. Buckeye Lake; appropriated amount not confirmed.
- **Connecticut DEEP** – $10M bond commission approval (2026) for state parks/trails/dams; HHPD FY2024 $3.04M; dam list not found.
- **Oregon** – No standing state program found; Wallowa Lake Dam funded ad hoc via lottery bonds, Business Oregon, OWRD, ODFW, NFWF and FY2022 HHPD.
